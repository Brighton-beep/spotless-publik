## Spotless 1.0.1

- Podcasts: when an episode ends (or you skip), the next one is picked for you — the show's next episode first, then new episodes from shows you follow, then something popular in the same genre. The queue shows why each one is there.
- Smoother chapter scrubbing: the slider follows your finger, chapter starts are gentle detents, and it only settles onto a chapter when you let go right next to it.
- Full-screen lyrics on iPhone now have previous and next buttons.
- Swipe from the left edge to go back on iPhone.
